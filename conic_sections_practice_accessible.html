Conic Sections (or Conics)

PARABOLA

A parabola is the set of all points in a plane equidistant from a fixed point F (the focus) and a fixed line l (the directrix).

Notes_for_Conics.pdf - source page 2 Ellipse

ELLIPSE

An ellipse is the set of all points in a plane, the sum of whose distances from two fixed points (called the foci) is a constant.

The foci lie on the longer axis, called the major axis. The endpoints of the major axis are called the vertices of the ellipse. The shorter axis is called the minor axis.

Standard Equations of an Ellipse with Center at the Origin

\[ \frac{x^2}{a^2}+\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1\qquad\text{or}\qquad\frac{x^2}{b^2}+\frac{y^2}{a^2}=1 \]

The graph, where a > b > 0, is an ellipse with center at the origin. The length of the major axis is 2a, and the length of the minor axis is 2b. The foci are a distance c from the origin, where c squared equals a squared minus b squared.

\[ a>b>0,\qquad c^2=a^2-b^2 \]

Equation of an Ellipse with Center at \((h,k)\)

\[ \frac{(x-h)^2}{a^2}+\frac{(y-k)^2}{b^2}=1 \]

Note that if center shifts, the foci will shift as well.

Notes_for_Conics.pdf - source page 3 Hyperbola

HYPERBOLA

A hyperbola is the set of all points in a plane, the difference of whose distances from two fixed points (the foci) is a positive constant.

Standard Equations of a Hyperbola with Center at the Origin

\[ \frac{x^2}{a^2}-\frac{y^2}{b^2}=1\qquad\text{or}\qquad\frac{y^2}{a^2}-\frac{x^2}{b^2}=1 \]

The graph is a hyperbola with center at the origin. The length of the transverse axis is 2a, and the length of the conjugate axis is 2b. The foci are a distance c from the origin, where c squared equals a squared plus b squared.

\[ c^2=a^2+b^2 \]

Equation of a Hyperbola with Center at \((h,k)\)

\[ \frac{(x-h)^2}{a^2}-\frac{(y-k)^2}{b^2}=1\qquad\text{or}\qquad\frac{(y-k)^2}{a^2}-\frac{(x-h)^2}{b^2}=1 \]
